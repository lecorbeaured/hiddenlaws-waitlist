Who it's for

For people who did everything right and still feel behind

You've read the money advice. You've built the budget, automated the savings, cut the subscriptions. And somehow the people who seem to break through aren't doing anything you haven't already tried.

The Hidden Laws of Money doesn't hand you an eleventh tip to add to the pile. It argues that the visible rules, the ones printed in every finance blog, were never the real ones, and it spends ten chapters showing you what was actually being taught in the households where wealth was already normal.

Then it hands you a 36-page workbook and asks you to apply what you've read before the feeling fades, inside the first 72 hours.

A note before you start

This book examines the psychology and unwritten social rules around money. It is not financial, legal, or investment advice, and it isn't a substitute for guidance from a licensed professional about your specific situation.
